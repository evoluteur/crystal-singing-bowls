Crystal Singing Bowls

Sing a bowl of pure quartz

Seven frosted quartz bowls, one for each chakra. Tap a bowl to strike it, or switch to Sing and hold it down to draw out the long, pure tone that a suede mallet makes circling the rim. Watch the water in the bowl shiver with the sound. Use headphones or good speakers.

About crystal bowls

The bowls are synthesized in your browser: a nearly pure tone with a faint beating overtone, like fused quartz.
